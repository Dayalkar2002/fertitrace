Recommended FERTITRACE Design Principle
Specimen ID (SP000789) should be treated as the primary traceability identifier rather than relying only on the Case/Patient ID. This allows FERTITRACE to maintain traceability as material moves through OPU → Oocyte → Fertilisation → Zygote → Embryo → Biopsy → Cryo → Thaw → Transfer.
Security Note
Avoid putting patient name, mobile number, address, Aadhaar/passport number, diagnosis, medical history, embryo grading, genetic results or other sensitive clinical information directly into the QR. The QR should identify the record; the FERTITRACE database should contain the detailed clinical and traceability information.
